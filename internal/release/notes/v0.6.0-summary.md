# Search

Search is now in the TUI. Press `Ctrl+K` from any screen, `/` when you're not typing, or click **Search** in the right sidebar. One list shows developers, organizations, commits, and actions, so you can jump to a profile, open a thread, or start a commit from the same place. **See all** opens every matching commit on its own screen.

The last three searches stay for your account. Click **Clear** or press `Ctrl+L` to forget them.

# Suggestions while you write

- Type `@` in the composer, a reply, a quote, a poll, or a Remotes message to get people and organizations to mention. In a thread, the people already talking show first.
- Type `#` and an issue number to find that issue and insert its link.
- Hashtags in commits and messages are links now. Click one to search for it.

# Commit shortcuts

- `Q` opens the commit a quote points to.
- `T` copies the text of your own commits, and of commits from organizations you post as.
- **Copy link** (`y`) shows on every commit, and every copy now confirms it with a toast.

# Achievements

Badges on a profile now show their icons, like the web. Press `A` or click the badge row to see every achievement, including the ones you haven't unlocked yet with your progress. Pick one to read what it takes.

# Smaller changes

- In an org's Remotes conversation, avatars show who pulled the latest message.
- An organization's admins show their avatars.
- Your counts, your live state, and **Live now** in the sidebars update as new commits arrive.

# Bug fixes

- ⚡ and other emoji now draw in color.
- On a profile with a tall header, `j` scrolls to the pinned commit instead of skipping it.
