# Six new themes

Press `F2` to pick one of six new themes:

- **Rosé Pine** and **Rosé Pine Dawn**, the dark and light halves of the soft rose palette.
- **GitHub Light**, the colors of github.com in light mode.
- **Ayu Light**, warm and bright.
- **Nord**, cool arctic blues.
- **Kanagawa**, the Wave variant, inspired by Hokusai's print.

# Mentions follow renames

A mention now opens the person or organization it named when the commit was written, even if they changed their handle since. Before, a renamed mention opened nobody, or whoever took the old handle. Mentions in Remotes messages work the same way.

# Swatches from hashtags

When a draft has a hashtag that is also a color, such as `#fade`, the composer shows "For a swatch, use color(#fade)." Press `Ctrl+Y` or click **Use as color** to turn it into a swatch. It works in commits, replies, quotes and Remotes.

# Guild icons in commits

Guild hashtags and the **posted in** and **because you joined** lines show the guild's uploaded icon, like the web. The emoji stays until the icon loads, or when images are off.
