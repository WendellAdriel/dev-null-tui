# Moderation history

Your profile now shows your moderation record under the counts: how many timeouts and bans you've had. Only you and site moderators see it. Press `M` or click the line to see each one with its reason and dates.

# New notifications

- **Timeouts** say why, for how long, and when you can post again.
- **First commits** and **level ups** get their own rows, like the web.
- Notifications about an organization's commits and mentions name the organization, as in "starred @acme's commit".

# Post as

The composer shows a **Post as** chip for `Ctrl+A` when you're a member of an organization. The list shows your organizations, and the old typed-handle fallback is gone.

# The TUI says who it is

Logging in and every signed request now name the app, so **Settings** > **Devices** on the web shows "dev-null-tui". Thanks to Matthieu Napoli (@mnapoli) for the idea and the first version.

# Bug fixes

- Orgs in Remotes can hold up to 256 people, matching the server.
- In pastéis mode, `\pastel` shows as plain "pastel", like the web.
