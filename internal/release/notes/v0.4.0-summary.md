# Remotes

Remotes, the direct messages and orgs from the web, are now in the TUI. Press `g r` or pick **Remotes** in the sidebar. It shows only when Remotes is turned on in Settings, with your unread count beside it.

The inbox has the web's **Open**, **Fetched**, and **Stashed** tabs, and `/` searches them. From 120 columns, the inbox and the conversation sit side by side. Narrower terminals show one at a time.

Conversations look like they do on the web: day markers, your messages on the right, images, link cards, replies, reactions, and the typing line. Reading a conversation marks it read only while it's on screen and scrolled to the newest message.

# Writing messages

- Press `w` or `Enter` to write, and `Enter` to push. `Ctrl+O` attaches up to 3 images.
- A refused message stays as **rejected** with the server's reason. `r` pushes it again, and the server never stores it twice. `d` discards it.
- Each conversation keeps its own draft, even after you quit. Drafts are deleted when you push, disconnect, or the device is revoked.
- `j` and `k` select a message. `r` replies, `1` to `3` react with the quick reactions, `e` opens the picker, and `p` pings.

# Voice notes

Select a voice note and press `Space` to play it. `Left` and `Right` seek, `x` switches between 1×, 1.5×, and 2×, and `-` and `+` change the volume.

Press `Ctrl+T` to record up to a minute, then listen to it before you push it. On Linux this works with PulseAudio or PipeWire. On macOS and Windows, recording needs `ffmpeg` or `sox`. Audio stays in memory and never touches the disk.

# Managing Remotes

- Merge (`m`) or drop (`d`) a message request.
- Start a conversation with `N`, or create an org with `O`.
- In an org, `I` opens its members. Maintainers can rename the org, add people, change roles, and remove people.
- Pinned messages show above the org's conversation, and `L` lists them. Maintainers pin and unpin with `!`.
- `.` opens the conversation's actions: profile, pin, stash, mute, and block or leave.

# Link cards

Commits and quoted commits now show the link card from the web: the site, the title, a short description, and the picture when your terminal supports graphics.

# Updates

devnull now checks for a new release every time it starts, not once a day. **Skip this version** still hides a release you don't want.
