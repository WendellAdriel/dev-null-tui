# Custom themes

You can make your own themes now. Press `F2` to open the theme picker, then `n` for a new theme. It starts from the theme you're using, **Default dark** or **Default light**, and you change its colors from there.

- Each color has a hex field. `#1e1e2e`, `1e1e2e` and the short `#abc` all work.
- Press `Enter` on a color to adjust it with hue, saturation and lightness sliders. Use `←/→` to move a slider, `Shift` for steps of ten, or click the bar. You can still type hex.
- The preview updates as you go, and a ◂ marks the parts the selected color changes.
- The editor tells you when a color pair is hard to read, but it doesn't stop you from saving.

Your themes show under **Custom themes** in the picker. Press `e` to edit one or `D` to delete it. You can keep up to 20.

# Desktop notifications

When the terminal isn't focused, devnull tells your desktop about new notifications and new Remotes messages, with the sender and a bit of the message. Muted conversations and the thread you have open stay quiet. Turn it off in **Settings** > **TUI** > **Desktop notifications**.

# Who reacted

In a Remotes thread, press `E` on a message to see who reacted, under each emoji. Press `Enter` to open someone's profile. A **Reactions** chip shows in the status bar when the selected message has reactions.
