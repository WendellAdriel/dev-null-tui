# Settings

Open **Settings** with `g ,` or from the sidebar. It has five tabs:

- **Profile** updates your details, syncs GitHub, picks your lane color, and turns pastéis mode on or off. It also holds Remotes preferences and permanent account deletion.
- **Region** sets your timezone, local time on your profile, and the language for translations.
- **Appearance** switches between light and dark for this session.
- **Safety** lists the people you muted or blocked. Mute with `M` and block with `B` from a profile, and report a commit with `!`.
- **Devices** lists your approved devices and revokes any of them, including this one.

Dialogs for destructive actions have a red border, and mute and report dialogs have a yellow one.

# What's new

This summary shows once after each upgrade. Scroll with `↑` and `↓`, and close it with `Esc`.

# Sidebars

- The left sidebar shows the /dev/null logo in dark and light versions.
- The right sidebar lines up your name and handle beside your avatar and shows the app version at the bottom.

# Pastéis

In pastéis mode, the pastel de nata from the web replaces the `◍` symbol beside commit counts, in the right sidebar, and on profiles. Terminals without image support keep the symbol.
