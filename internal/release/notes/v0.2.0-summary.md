# Settings

Press `g ,` or pick **Settings** in the sidebar. You get six tabs:

- **Profile**: edit your details, sync GitHub, pick your lane color, toggle pastéis mode, set Remotes preferences, or delete your account.
- **Region**: your timezone, local time on your profile, and the translation language.
- **Appearance**: light or dark for this session.
- **Safety**: the people you muted or blocked. Mute (`M`) and block (`B`) from a profile, and report a commit with `!`.
- **Devices**: your approved devices. Revoke any of them, including this one.
- **TUI**: settings for this app. For now, the daily update check.

Destructive dialogs now have a red border, and mute and report dialogs a yellow one.

# Updates

Once a day, devnull checks for a new release when it starts. When there is one, you can update and restart right away, wait, or skip that version.

# What's new

That's this screen! It shows once after each upgrade. Scroll with `↑` and `↓`, and close it with `Esc`.

# Sidebars

- The left sidebar shows the /dev/null logo, in dark and light versions.
- The right sidebar puts your name and handle beside your avatar, and the app version at the bottom.

# Commit bodies

Commits now look like they do on the web:

- Links are shortened and stay clickable. GitHub, GitLab, and Codeberg links show the repository and the issue or pull request, and dev/null commit links show the author and hash.
- Mentions link to profiles. Quotes, emoji shortcodes, hex colors, and the /dev/null wordmark get their own styling.
- Code blocks show their file name, with syntax highlighting.

# Pastéis

In pastéis mode, the pastel de nata from the web replaces the `◍` symbol on commits, in the sidebar, and on profiles. It also replaces the words pastel and pastéis in commits.
