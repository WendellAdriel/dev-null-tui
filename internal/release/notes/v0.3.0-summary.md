# Polls

Commits now show their polls below the text, the same way the web does. Until you vote, an open poll shows its options and the time left. After that, you see a bar and percentage for each option, with a check on the one you picked.

To vote, press `o` or click **Vote**, choose an option, and press `Enter`. You can also click an option directly. Votes are final, so the confirmation starts on **Cancel** and `y` records the vote.

To create a poll, press `Ctrl+L` in the composer. A poll has 2 to 4 options and lasts 1 hour, 1 day, 3 days, or 7 days. Polls can't go on replies, on quotes, or with images.

# Images

A commit can now carry up to 3 JPEG, PNG, or WebP images.

- Press `Ctrl+O` to pick them from a file browser inside the composer. It lists only folders and images, shows a thumbnail of the selected image, and remembers the last folder you used.
- You can also drag image files into the terminal.
- The attachment strip below the editor shows a thumbnail of each image. `Tab` moves to it, where `Enter` adds a description and `Alt+↑` and `Alt+↓` reorder images.
- Preview draws your images as thumbnails when your terminal supports graphics.

With images attached, the text is optional.

# Organizations

Commits can now come from organizations. Organization profiles aren't available yet, so those commits have no **Profile** action, and `u` does nothing on them.

# Bug fixes

- Long commits now show their full text. The log shows the server's excerpt first, like the web, and `m` or a click on **Show more** unfolds the rest. Opening a thread shows its commit whole.
- Replying from a log, Starred, or a profile now shows the reply under its commit right away, without a refresh.
