# Hot topics

See what people commit about. Hot topics show in the right column, one card per topic, and **Hot topics** sits in the sidebar above Settings.

- Press `H` to pick a topic. Its whole recap shows under the list.
- Press `g h` to open the Hot topics screen, with every topic and its recap.
- Opening a topic lists its commits, where you can star, reply and open threads as usual.

# More replies under each commit

Logs, profiles and Starred now show up to 3 of the newest replies under each commit, like the web. Change it in **Settings** > **Appearance** > **Replies**: 1, 3, 5, 10, 15, 20 or 25.

When a commit has more replies than that, a **N more replies** row ends the list. Press `Enter` on the selected commit, or click the row, to open the thread with every reply.

# Report bugs and request features

Start a new commit with `/bug ` or `/feature `, or pick **Report a bug** or **Request a feature** in search. The composer turns into an issue form with the web's template, and `Ctrl+S` opens it on GitHub. Your terminal, OS, window size, language, color scheme and current screen go with it, so the bug is easier to reproduce.

The **Opened issue** notice stays for 10 seconds. Press `Ctrl+O` or click it to open the issue.
