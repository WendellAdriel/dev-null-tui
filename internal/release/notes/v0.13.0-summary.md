# Guilds

Guilds are here, matching the web. Press `g u` or pick **Guilds** in the sidebar to browse and search the directory, then open a guild's board.

- **Boards** show the pinned commit first, then the newest commits that mention the guild. A reply that mentions it shows who it answers, and `Enter` opens its full thread.
- **Post** with `c` on a board. The post stays in the guild instead of your profile log, and you can schedule it.
- **Join**, **leave**, **mute**, **report**, and see the **members** (`U`).
- **Create** a guild, or edit one you moderate, with an emoji or an uploaded icon. Moderators pin top-level commits with `b`.
- **Your guilds** sit in the sidebar with their missed commits. `g 1` to `g 5` open the first five, and `g m` lists them all.
- Hashtags that name a guild draw as its emoji and name, and a click opens the board.

# Half-year activity grid

Profiles with a half-year of activity show a 26-week grid with month and day labels, like the web. Hover over or click a day to see its count.

# Jump to the top with `Z`

`Z` puts the focused commit at the top of the screen in the Log, Starred, notifications, profiles, boards and threads, so you can keep reading from there. Thanks to Eric (@ericvanjohnson) for the idea.

# Ocean Drive theme

A new dark theme inspired by the GTA VI trailers: a pink-to-orange sunset over deep blue, with teal water and palm green. Press `F2` to pick it.

# Faster images

Avatars and guild icons no longer wait behind photos, so they show up sooner.
