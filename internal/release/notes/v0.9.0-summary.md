# Themes

There are more themes now: **Dark**, **Light**, **Catppuccin Latte**, **Catppuccin Mocha**, **Dracula**, **Solarized Light**, **Synthwave '84**, **Tokyo Night** and **Tokyo Night Day**. Each one has its own look, so there's no separate light and dark mode to pick anymore. **Auto** is still there, and it follows your terminal's background.

Press `F2` from anywhere in the workspace to open the theme picker, or click **Theme** in **Actions**. You can also pick one in **Settings** > **TUI**. The **Mode** row in **Appearance** is gone. `--theme auto`, `--theme light` and `--theme dark` still work for one run.

If you saved a theme and mode in v0.8.0, it becomes the matching theme the first time you open v0.9.0. Event themes now use their dark or light colors to match the theme you picked.

# Chips everywhere

- The `g` menu in the status bar shows each place as a chip you can click.
- In Remotes, the status bar is chips too: inbox, thread, voice note, search and **Write to**. **Ping** moved there, and **Write** and **Actions** moved to the composer row.
- Notification filters are chips, and a filter that's off has a dimmed label.
- **Actions** and **Shortcuts** in the right sidebar are full-width chips, with the key on the right.
- Hints in popups, composers and the image browser are chips you can click. A chip only shows when its key works where you are.

# Smaller changes

- Hints show `↑/↓` and `←/→` instead of `j/k` and `left/right`. The old keys still work.
- On a narrow terminal, the chips at the end of the status bar drop first.

# Bug fixes

- `g` shortcuts now take you out of **Settings**.
