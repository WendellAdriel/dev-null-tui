# Themes

The TUI has themes now. Open **Settings**, go to the **TUI** tab, and pick one under **Theme**. For now there's **Default** plus the event themes, and more TUI themes are coming. Your theme and Auto, Light or Dark mode are saved, so they stay the same the next time you open the TUI. `F2` still switches dark and light, and that's saved too. `--theme` overrides the mode for one run.

The web's event themes are here too: Halloween, Christmas and New Year, with their own colors and a few touches like the Santa hat on the logo and a bat flying by on wide screens. In **Settings** > **Appearance**, **Event theme** can follow the web's dates with **Auto** (Halloween from Oct 29 to Nov 1, Christmas from Dec 23 to 26, New Year from Dec 30 to Jan 2), stay on **Default**, or keep one event on. While an event theme is active, it overrides your theme.

# Drafts

Closing the composer with `Ctrl+K` keeps your draft, and now it's saved on disk, so it's still there after you quit. Commits, replies and quotes each get their own draft, with their images and polls, and opening the composer in the same place brings it back.

To see them all, click **Drafts** in the left sidebar or press `g d`. Replies and quotes show who you're answering and a bit of their commit. Press `Enter` to open a draft or `D` to delete it.

# More actions for a commit

The **⋯ More** chip, or `.`, opens a menu with everything that doesn't fit in the chips: copy the link or text, open a link, send the commit to someone in Remotes, see who starred it, pin it, follow, mute or block the author, and delete or report it. Every shortcut you already use still works.

# Who to follow

Find people to follow, like on the web. Press `w`, or click **Who to follow** in **Actions**, to open a list of suggestions. Press `f` to follow someone, `X` if you're not interested, `R` for other suggestions, and `Enter` to open a profile. On narrow terminals, the suggestions show in your Following and Latest logs after the 5th commit.

# Avatars

Click an avatar to see it in full size, in logs, threads, profiles, notifications, followers lists and Remotes. On a profile, `V` opens it too.

# Smaller changes

- In a long Remotes conversation, **↓ Back to present** takes you to the newest message, and it counts what's new while you're scrolled up. `G` does the same.
- The action chips from commits are now used everywhere else too: profiles and organizations, people lists, notifications, the image viewer, **Go live**, and Remotes. Each shows its key, and you can click it. Notification actions also sit under a line, like on commits.
- The Remotes hints under the composer are chips now, and so are **Retry** and **Discard** on a message that failed to send.
- The right sidebar fits short terminals better.
