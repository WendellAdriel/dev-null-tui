# Organizations

Organization pages are now in the TUI. Press `u` or click **Profile** on an organization's commit to open its page, with its links, followers, admins, and the **Commits** and **Mentions** tabs. Press `f` to follow it. Your Following list shows the organizations you follow above the people.

To post as an organization you're a member of, press `Ctrl+A` in the composer and pick it.

# Go live

Press `L` to go live. Paste your YouTube or Twitch link and choose how many hours to show it, up to 12. While you're live, your avatar gets a red ring and your commits say **LIVE**. Press `L` again to end the stream. The aside's **Live now** shows up to three people you follow who are streaming, and a click opens the stream.

# New commits

Following and Latest now check for new commits every 15 seconds and place them above the ones you're reading, without moving you. If you're not at the top, a **New commits** button shows who posted. Click it or scroll up to read them.

While the terminal isn't focused, the log, notifications, and Remotes check every 30 seconds. `Ctrl+R` now refreshes the notification and Remotes badges along with the log.

# Smaller changes

- Your counts and pastéis moved to the left sidebar, and the right sidebar now shows only actions and shortcuts.
- On a profile, `F` opens Followers and `O` opens Following.
- Voice notes have more contrast, so the waveform is easy to read on your own messages.
- Releases now include versioned archives beside the plain executables.

# Bug fixes

- The commit activity bars are all the same width now, like the web.
- On Windows, images dropped into the terminal as file links now attach.
