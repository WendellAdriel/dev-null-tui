# Scheduled commits

You can now write a commit and send it later. In the composer, press `Ctrl+T` to pick a time in your local timezone, up to 30 days ahead. The **Submit** button becomes **Schedule**. It works when you post as an organization too. Replies and quotes can't be scheduled.

Your queue has its own place in the left sidebar: click **Scheduled** or press `g t`. Only you can see it. Press `Enter` to commit one right away, or `D` to cancel it.

# More you can do with a commit

- `P` pins your commit to your profile, or unpins it. If you already have a pinned commit, you're asked before it's replaced.
- Deleted something by mistake? You have 30 seconds to press `z`, or click the toast, and it's back where it was.
- `t` translates a commit into the language from your Region settings. Press `t` again to see the original.
- On your own commits, `S` or a click on the star count shows who starred it.
- A thread now shows related commits below it. Move to one with `j` and open it with `Enter`.

# Links

Links in commits are clickable now, and so are `@mentions`, which open the profile right in the TUI. Press `x` to open a commit's link, and when there's more than one, pick from a list. It works on Remotes messages too.

# Profiles

Mentions in a bio or organization show as chips with the person's avatar, like the web. They open the dev/null profile when there is one, or GitHub when there isn't. Hashtags in a bio open search.

# Smaller changes

- `W` watches someone who's live from almost anywhere. When more than one person is live, you pick who.
- Commit shortcuts now show as chips under the commit.
- The selected notification shows **Open** next to **Profile**, and both are clickable.
- The **Commit** button in the sidebar has more room around it.
- Lists and menus like **Members**, **React**, and **All achievements** now have the same blue border as the other dialogs.
